About our college

Our college is a vibrant hub of learning, growth, and community where academic excellence meets a supportive and lively campus environment. Equipped with modern classrooms, a well-stocked library, and active student spaces, the institution encourages curiosity both inside and outside the lecture hall. Dedicated professors guide students with a passion for teaching, while a diverse student body makes every day an opportunity to share new perspectives and build lasting friendships. Whether participating in cultural events, sports, or student clubs, campus life strikes a balanced and memorable path toward personal and professional success.

Pragnya Degree & PG College, located near the Gandhi Statue in Chanda Nagar, Hyderabad, was established in 2010 as a private, un-aided institution affiliated with Osmania University. Founded by the Pragnya Group of Colleges under the leadership of Chairman S. Sreenivasa Reddy, the institution was built on the core motto of delivering quality, affordable higher education tailored to modern industrial requirements. Following its initial launch, the group expanded its footprint in the locality by establishing the Pragnya Womens Degree College in 2011 to offer dedicated educational pathways for female students. Over the years, the campus has evolved into a well-recognized multi-faceted institution offering diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs—such as BBA, BCA, B.Com, and B.Sc—while building a reputation for active student engagement, campus infrastructure, and structured career placement support
