Pragnya Degree & PG College is a private, co-educational and women's degree institute located in Chanda Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana. Backed by a student intake capacity of around 720 students, the campus features essential infrastructure such as computer and science laboratories, a library, projector-equipped classrooms, and placement and skill development support.
